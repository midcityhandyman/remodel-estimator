ASSUMPTIONS (catalog defaults used in this estimate; check them against how you build)
  * Plumbing fixtures with a rough-in part are two visits: the valve body goes in at rough-in,
    the trim and shower head after the tile is grouted.
  * Electrical is two visits: rough-in (boxes, cable, fan housing and duct) before insulation
    and drywall, then trim-out (devices, plates, light fixtures, fan grille) after paint and
    tile. About two thirds of the device labor is rough-in. The trim-out visit carries its own
    half hour of setup.
  * Drywall is five visits: hang, tape coat, second coat, finish coat, sand, with one dry day
    after each coat (all purpose premixed compound at normal room conditions). Ceilings are hung
    before walls. Compound is one 4.5 gallon bucket per 450 sf across the three coats.
  * Backer board screws: coverage assumes a box of 185 screws, 1-1/4 inch. Walls take about 2
    screws per sf (8 inches on center into studs at 16 inches), so one box covers about 90 sf.
    Floors take about 3.7 per sf (8 inches on center both ways), so one box covers about 50 sf.
    Seams are mesh taped and filled from the tile thinset.
  * Floor tile underlayment default: 1/4 inch cement backer board bedded in thinset over a sound
    wood subfloor, screwed 8 inches on center, seams mesh taped and filled with thinset. It goes
    down after paint, right before the floor tile. If you use an uncoupling membrane instead,
    swap this item for your own.
  * Trim arrives primed and is installed after the walls are painted, so it gets its own paint
    visit: fill nail holes, caulk, two finish coats.
  * Hall bath: baseboard is 10 lf: the 26 lf perimeter less 3 lf for 1 door opening at 3 lf
    each, 10 lf for shower alcove, 3 lf for vanity.

ASSUMPTIONS (catalog defaults used in this estimate; check them against how you build)
  * Walls on a slab: pressure treated bottom plate over sill sealer, pinned with concrete

    before walls. Compound is one 4.5 gallon bucket per 450 sf across the three coats.
